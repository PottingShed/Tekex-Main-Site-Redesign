Taxteq

Two TEKEX companies. One joint venture.

Where TEKEX came in. A joint venture of two Jersey TEKEX companies: Boxteq, maker of the aviation-inspired Black Box for Business®, and Safelink, a decade into encrypted data rooms.

Case 03Global · Health technology

Parent Sense

Making sense of year one.

Sense-IT guides new parents through the first, often overwhelming, year. Parents log their baby’s day in the Parent Sense app and get routines and expert advice made for that baby.

Where TEKEX came in. Parent Sense took its pitch to the TEKEX investor network, and research is under way into how its data could cut healthcare costs and improve maternal and child health.
