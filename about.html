About TEKEX 01

Connection
should not be
left to chance.

TEKEX brings founders, investors and business leaders together across Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man, helping innovative businesses find the capital, expertise and connections to grow.

Why we exist 02

The ingredients are here.
They need connecting.

The Crown Dependencies do not lack ambition, capital or expertise. But too often, the people building businesses and the people who could help them grow operate in separate networks.

TEKEX exists to change that. We make innovation visible, create opportunities for people to meet and help good businesses find the people and resources they need for their next stage.

How we work 03

The event is the beginning.
Not the end.

Events give people a reason to meet. The wider value comes through the investment conversations, introductions, shared expertise and partnerships that follow.

Make innovation visible.

Give ambitious businesses a platform to share what they are building, and bring fresh ideas and perspectives into the islands.

Bring people together.

Create opportunities for founders, investors, business leaders and advisers to meet, ask questions and explore where they can help one another.

Help businesses move forward.

Connect businesses with the capital, customers, mentors, expertise and partnerships that can help them take their next step.

Our ambition 04

Three islands.
A bigger opportunity.

Connecting Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man gives our businesses access to a network larger than any one island, with the collective reach to open doors further afield.
