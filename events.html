04/11/26/17:00

TEKEX IOM3

Global Innovation. Isle of Man Opportunity.

Isle of Man/Santander Work Cafe

Register → for TEKEX IOM3 on Eventbrite (opens in a new tab)

TEKEX is back in the Isle of Man, this time to identify global innovations that could become valuable Isle of Man opportunities, then connect each one with the local investment, partners and decision-makers required to launch it.

Jason Bissell, exited founder and now entrepreneur, will be speaking on Innovation Islands and what’s next for the Crown. Mark Sutcliffe will deliver the keynote on connecting ambitious innovation with agile jurisdictions, in his talk ‘The Billion Dollar Exit to Building in the Crown’.

You'll also see a panel debate and have the chance to put your questions to founders.

All ticket proceeds go to Craig’s Heartstrong Foundation.

Line-up

  • Jason BissellExited founder and entrepreneur — Innovation Islands
  • Mark SutcliffeKeynote — The Billion Dollar Exit to Building in the Crown
  • Josh DawkinsR&D Manager and Head of Production, iO-CO
  • Claire OwenCEO and Co-founder, Pupil Power
  • Will TinneyCEO and Founder, Lusyi
